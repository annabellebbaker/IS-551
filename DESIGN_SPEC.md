# Financial Literacy App - Design Specification

---

## 🎯 Affordance Sentence (The One Thing Users Must See First)

> **Learn financial literacy from real professionals in 5-minute lessons. Get ahead, invest confidently, earn certifications.**

This is the dominant message on the landing screen—nothing competes with this until the user understands what they can do here.

---

## Product Overview

**Product Name:** [To be determined]

**Purpose:** Empower young adults (college students and early-career professionals) to make confident financial decisions through short, interactive learning modules delivered by real financial professionals.

---

## Problem Statement

**Need:** Students need reliable ways to learn financial content in short snippets to increase financial literacy. Many college students and young working professionals lack understanding of key financial terms (mortgage, 401k, Roth IRA). Currently, they rely on social media and family education, costing them years of potential investment growth and financial progress.

---

## Target User Profile

**Primary Persona:** 
- College students looking to get ahead on finances
- Young adults entering the workforce early in their careers
- Gen Z and Gen Alpha with limited/no personal finance management experience
- Users who feel overwhelmed by financial complexity

---

## Core Capability

**What Users Can Do:**
- Get ahead on financial terminology and concepts
- Put money confidently into their future through a reliable, expert-curated source
- Access professional financial expertise without overwhelming complexity
- Progress through structured learning at their own pace

---

## Value Proposition

**Material & Emotional Benefits:**
- Invest faster into future endeavors
- Set themselves up for retirement success
- Gain confidence to teach others about investing
- Eliminate pressure/shame about being "behind" on financial knowledge
- Make informed, accountable decisions about their financial future

---

## Three Core Screens

### Screen 1: Landing/Home Screen

**Main Job:** Introduce the brand identity and encourage entry into the learning experience

**Design Description:** 
A welcoming homepage featuring the product name as the focal point, set against a vintage schoolhouse-themed design. The layout is simple and uncluttered, showcasing learning module categories without overwhelming the user. Instead of the traditional "finance green," the design embraces a warm, retro color palette that creates positive learning nostalgia and signals a place for growth.

**Why It Earned This Slot:** 
Establishes brand trust and clarity—users immediately understand what the app is and why they're here.

**Design Question Answered:** 
"What is this app, and why should I trust it?"

**Key Visual Elements:**
- Product name (cursive, ADA adaptable) prominently displayed
- Clear value proposition statement
- Visual module category cards in warm, vintage colors
- Brick red and cream as primary accent colors

---

### Screen 2: Learning Module

**Main Job:** Demonstrate the learning experience and show how knowledge becomes actionable

**Design Description:** 
An interactive learning module for a specific financial term. The screen includes: a progress bar at the top showing module completion status, a clear definition of the term, embedded expert video recordings, explanations of future value impact, and recorded walkthroughs showing immediate practical application. Content is chunked into small, manageable sections that encourage completion.

**Why It Earned This Slot:** 
Shows the core product working—education delivered in short, digestible bites with professional expertise and real-world applicability. This is where the value is delivered.

**Design Question Answered:** 
"What does learning look like here, and can I actually use this information right away?"

**Key Visual Elements:**
- Progress bar (green for completed sections, muted color for incomplete)
- Video player for expert content
- Definition section with clear typography
- "Try It" or application section showing real-world use
- Navigation to next lesson

---

### Screen 3: Progress & Achievement Dashboard

**Main Job:** Visualize progress, reward completion, and motivate continued learning

**Design Description:** 
A personalized dashboard showing earned certifications, a visual breakdown of module progress across different financial topics, and encouragement through progressive unlocking (basic modules visible first, advanced modules revealed as users level up). The screen celebrates how far users have come and awards them through visual progress indicators and possible rewards/badges.

**Why It Earned This Slot:** 
Demonstrates the motivational and reward system that keeps users engaged. Shows material evidence of progress and achievement—this is where users feel empowered and celebrated.

**Design Question Answered:** 
"How am I progressing, and am I being rewarded for my effort?"

**Key Visual Elements:**
- Certification badges earned
- Module progress dashboard (visual chart or grid)
- Level progression indicator
- Celebration/encouragement messaging
- Teaser for locked/upcoming modules
- Profile adjustment option (secondary feature)

---

## Design System

### Tone & Voice
**Chill, approachable, retro schoolhouse vibe** — Fun yet professional. Avoiding corporate finance language; using conversational, encouraging tone.

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Brick red, emerald/muted green, warm cream
- **Secondary:** Clean whites and neutrals
- **Success State:** Green (for progress, completed items, positive actions)
- **Overall Aesthetic:** Vintage schoolhouse warmth

### Typography
- **Headings & Product Name:** Simple cursive (must be ADA adaptable for accessibility and readability)
- **Body Text:** Times New Roman OR clean Sans Serif font
- **Hierarchy:** Clear distinction between heading and body through weight and size

### Visual Style
- Vintage schoolhouse theme with retro inspiration
- Warm, nostalgic color blocking
- Clear, organized layouts avoiding clutter
- Professional iconography with a retro twist
